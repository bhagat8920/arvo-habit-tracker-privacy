Privacy Policy

ARVO Habit Tracker for Android

Last updated: November 17, 2026

ARVOSEC ("we", "us", or "our") publishes ARVO Habit Tracker ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. Information you enter into the App

The App lets you create and manage information such as habits, schedules, one-time tasks, notes, categories, preferences, and reminder settings. This information is stored locally on your device in the App's local database. The App does not require you to create an ARVO account to use these features.

Based on the App's current design, ARVOSEC does not operate a cloud account or synchronization service for this locally stored content.

2. Advertising and third-party services

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display banner and app-open advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information to provide, deliver, measure, and help prevent fraud in advertising. Depending on your device settings, location, applicable law, and consent choices, this information may include:

  • Advertising identifiers and other device or app identifiers;
  • IP address and general device/network information;
  • App interactions, ad impressions, and diagnostic or performance information; and
  • Information used to determine whether advertising can be personalized.

Google's processing is subject to Google's terms and privacy disclosures. For more information, review the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps or sites that use its services.

ARVOSEC does not sell the habit, task, or note content that you enter into the App.

3. Consent and privacy choices

The App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising-related consent where required. The message and choices shown to you may depend on your location and applicable privacy requirements.

Where a privacy-options entry point is made available in the App, you can use it to review or change the choices supported by the consent message. You can also review your device's advertising settings.

4. Local storage and backups

Your habits, tasks, notes, and app preferences are stored in the App's private storage on your device. The App includes a user-initiated JSON backup and restore feature.

  • A backup file is created when you choose to export it.
  • You choose the destination for the exported file.
  • You can select a backup file to restore information in the App.
  • Files exported to a location outside the App are not automatically deleted when you uninstall the App.

ARVOSEC does not provide cloud backup or synchronization for this information.

5. Reminders and notifications

The App can schedule local notifications for habits and tasks. These reminders are scheduled on your device. You can manage notification permission and notification display through your Android device settings.

6. How information is shared

ARVOSEC does not operate a server that receives or stores the habit, task, or note content you enter into the App. Information may be processed by Google through the advertising and consent services described in this Policy. Google may disclose information as described in its own privacy documentation and as permitted by applicable law.

7. Data retention and deletion

Information stored in the App's private local storage remains on your device until you delete it, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App. You can delete individual items using the App's available features.

Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage generally removes the App's private local data. You are responsible for deleting exported backup files from the locations where you saved them. Google handles advertising-related information under its own retention practices and policies.

8. Data security

The App stores its core habit-tracking information in private application storage on your device. Protect your device with an appropriate screen lock and keep exported backup files in a secure location. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not designed specifically for children and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising services may apply their own age-related settings and restrictions.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, third-party services, or applicable requirements change. The revised Policy will be published at this same URL with an updated revision date.

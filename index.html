Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026 · Last updated: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ARVO Habit Tracker handles information when you use the app. Read it together with the app’s Terms & Conditions.

1. Developer and contact

ARVO Habit Tracker is published by ARVOSEC (individual developer).

Privacy contact: info@arvosec.com

2. No ARVO account or ARVO-hosted sync

The app does not require an ARVO account and ARVOSEC does not operate an account system or its own cloud synchronisation service for your habit-tracking content.

Information you enter is primarily stored locally. However, Android backup and device-transfer features may copy eligible app data to a Google backup destination or another device. Therefore, “no ARVO-hosted sync” does not mean data can never leave your device.

3. Information stored by the app

Depending on the features you use, the app may store locally:

  • Habits, schedules, completion records and progress history.
  • Tasks, task status and related dates.
  • Notes and text you choose to enter.
  • Categories, quick links and preferences such as theme and reminder settings.
  • Operational settings used for reminders and advertising frequency controls.

Avoid entering information you do not want stored on the device or included in a backup.

4. Local storage

Habit, task, note and preference data is stored in the app’s local database in Android app-specific storage. ARVOSEC does not receive this content through an ARVO-operated server during normal app operation. Access to local data is subject to Android’s application sandbox and your device security. Anyone who gains access to your unlocked device or an accessible backup may be able to access it.

5. Android Auto Backup and device transfer

The app enables Android backup and data-extraction rules for selected app database files. Depending on Android version, device manufacturer, Google account and device settings, eligible app data may be included in Android cloud backup or device-to-device transfer.

  • Cloud backup: Eligible database files may be backed up by Android to the backup service associated with your device. Availability and behavior are controlled by Android, your device and account settings.
  • Encryption condition: On Android versions where the configured rules support it, cloud backup is restricted to devices reporting client-side encryption capability. This is a platform capability condition, not a guarantee that every backup destination or transfer is end-to-end encrypted.
  • Device transfer: Android may transfer eligible app data directly as part of device migration.
  • Excluded data: App rules are configured to exclude certain third-party SDK, cache and plugin data. Restored content can vary by Android version and backup path.

Manage or disable Android backup through your device and Google account settings. Removing the app from one device does not necessarily delete separate cloud backups or copies already transferred elsewhere. Manage those through the relevant Android or Google account controls.

6. User-created JSON backup and restore

The app provides a user-initiated backup and restore feature. When you export a backup, the app creates a file containing the user data selected by that feature, which may include habits, tasks, notes and related app content.

You choose where to save or share the file using Android’s file-selection and sharing interfaces. A copy saved outside the app is governed by the storage provider or service you choose. Keep backup files secure.

Restoring a backup imports data into the app and may replace or merge existing information depending on the restore operation presented. Review the confirmation shown by the app.

7. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

The app displays advertisements using the Google Mobile Ads SDK, including banner and full-screen formats where enabled. Google and its advertising partners may process information from the app or device to provide, measure, limit, prevent fraud in and improve advertising services.

Depending on the ad request, region, consent choices, device settings and Google services, information processed may include:

  • Advertising identifiers and other device or app identifiers.
  • IP address and general device or network information.
  • App interactions, ad impressions, diagnostics and performance information.
  • Information used for fraud prevention, security, frequency capping and advertising measurement.

ARVOSEC does not intend to send your habit entries, task descriptions or notes to advertising providers through ad placements. Advertising providers process information under their own privacy terms.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob.

8. Advertising consent and privacy choices

The app uses Google’s consent tools (UMP), where applicable, to determine whether advertising requests may be made and whether privacy choices are required in your region.

When Google requires a privacy-options entry point, the app provides a Privacy Settings option. Available choices may vary by region and applicable requirements. You can also manage advertising and privacy settings through Android and your Google account where those controls are available.

9. Notifications and reminders

If you enable reminders, the app may schedule local notifications on your device for habits or tasks. Notification permission is requested where Android requires it. Reminder scheduling information is used to deliver reminders you configure.

Notifications are handled through Android and the app’s notification plugin. The app does not need to send habit or task content to an ARVOSEC server to schedule local reminders. Manage notification permissions through Android Settings and reminder preferences within the app.

10. How information is used

Locally stored information is used to provide habit tracking, task management, notes, progress statistics, backup and restore, preferences and reminders. Advertising-related information is processed by advertising providers for the purposes described above. ARVOSEC does not sell your locally stored habit, task or note content.

11. Sharing and third-party services

ARVOSEC does not operate a backend service that routinely receives your habit, task or note content. Information may nevertheless be processed or transferred by:

  • Google Mobile Ads and related consent services for advertising and consent purposes.
  • Android backup, device-transfer, notification and operating-system services when you use or enable those platform features.
  • A file, cloud-storage, email or sharing provider you choose when exporting or sharing a backup.
  • Authorities or other parties where disclosure is required by applicable law.

Third-party services are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

12. Data retention and deletion

App content remains in the local database until you delete it, clear app storage, uninstall the app or otherwise remove it using Android or app-provided controls. The app may retain operational settings while installed to support its features.

Exported backup files remain wherever you save or share them until you delete those copies. Android or Google backup copies may be retained according to the backup service’s retention and deletion behavior. Copies transferred to another device must be managed there.

Because the app does not provide an ARVO account or ARVO-hosted content store, there is no ARVO account database to delete. To remove local app data, use Android’s app storage controls or uninstall the app. Separately manage backups and exported files with the relevant service or device.

13. Security

The app stores primary user content in Android app-specific storage and relies on Android’s application sandbox and device security features. Backup protection depends on Android version, device capabilities, account settings and backup destination. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Keep your operating system updated, use a secure screen lock and protect exported backup files.

14. Children’s privacy

ARVO Habit Tracker is not designed specifically for children under the age required by applicable law and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a feature or service, contact info@arvosec.com.

We may update this policy when app features, data practices or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. Continued use gives you an opportunity to review the revised policy; it does not replace any consent required separately by applicable law.
